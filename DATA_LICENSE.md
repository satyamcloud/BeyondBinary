# Data license and terms of use

**Scope.** This applies to `data/` (the 568-claim dataset and the Fact Crescendo
candidate URL list).

**Annotations and compilation** (the verdict-to-label mapping, the selection and
organisation of rows, and the dataset structure) are released by the authors under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode).
Please cite the accompanying paper when you use them.

**Third-party content.** The `text` column holds text of publicly posted social media
content that was embedded in fact-checking articles published by Alt News and Fact Crescendo.
The authors of that content, and the publishers of the articles, retain their rights. The CC BY 4.0
grant above does **not** extend to that third-party text. It is shared for non-commercial research
on misinformation detection only. The `url` column points to the source fact-check article.
Original article text and images are not included.

**Takedown.** If you are the author of a post, or a publisher, and want an entry removed,
write to satyam.sunderam@kiet.edu and it will be removed.

This file is a statement of the authors' intent, not legal advice.
